## 3. Gaps and Challenges

Based on an assessment of Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders, the business faces the following challenges:

### 3.1 Inconsistent and Non-Loyal Customers

The business does not have a constant or loyal customer base. Some customers may purchase from the business only occasionally and may switch to other shops.

### 3.2 Strong Competition from Nearby Shops

There are other shops located directly across from the business. These competitors sell similar products and compete for the same customers.

### 3.3 Slow and Limited Delivery

The business currently uses bicycles for delivery. This can make deliveries slower, especially when customers are located far away or when several deliveries need to be made.

### 3.4 Theft and Shop Security

The business sometimes experiences theft, where people take items from the shop without authorization. This can lead to financial losses and difficulty monitoring the shop at all times.

---

## 4. Possible Solutions for Each Challenge

### 4.1 Solution for Inconsistent and Non-Loyal Customers

The business can improve customer loyalty by providing excellent customer service and consistently selling quality products.

A digital customer loyalty system can also be introduced where customers are registered and rewarded for repeat purchases. Customers can earn points and receive discounts or rewards after reaching a certain number of points.

### 4.2 Solution for Competition from Nearby Shops

The business can introduce competitive discounts, promotions and special offers to encourage customers to continue buying from Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders instead of competing shops.

The business can also offer special prices to frequent customers through a loyalty program.

### 4.3 Solution for Slow Delivery

The business can improve delivery speed by working with boda boda riders for deliveries instead of depending only on bicycles.

Orders can be recorded digitally so that delivery requests can be organized and assigned to available riders.

### 4.4 Solution for Theft and Security

The business can employ security personnel to monitor the shop and protect business property.

The existing surveillance cameras should also be monitored regularly so that suspicious activities can be identified quickly.

## 5. Solution Selected to Build

### Customer Loyalty and Sales Management System

The solution selected for development is a **Customer Loyalty and Sales Management System** for Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders.

The main purpose of the system is to help the business attract customers, encourage repeat purchases and compete more effectively with nearby shops.

The system will allow the business to register customers, record sales, track customer purchases and provide rewards to loyal customers.

### Main Features

The proposed system will include:

- Customer registration and customer profiles
- Customer phone number management
- Recording of customer purchases
- Loyalty points for repeat purchases
- Discount management
- Promotional offers
- Customer purchase history
- Sales records
- Product and stock management
- Sales reports
- User login and access control
- Dashboard for monitoring business activities

### How the System Will Help Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders

The system will help the business identify regular customers and reward them for purchasing frequently.

For example, a customer can receive loyalty points whenever they make a purchase. After collecting enough points, the customer can redeem them for a discount.

The business can also create special promotions for loyal customers, helping to encourage repeat purchases and improve customer retention.

Sales records will allow the business owner to understand which products are selling well and which customers purchase frequently.

Overall, the system will help Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders build stronger relationships with customers and compete more effectively with nearby shops.

## 6. Research on Existing Similar Solutions in the Market

Several existing retail and Point-of-Sale (POS) systems already provide features related to customer management, loyalty, discounts and sales management.

### 6.1 Loyverse POS

Loyverse is a retail POS platform used by businesses in many countries. It provides sales management, inventory management, customer management and loyalty features.

Its loyalty program allows customers to earn points through purchases and redeem those points for discounts. It also supports customer purchase history and customer records.

This makes Loyverse similar to the proposed Rafik Wholesale and Retail Traders solution because both focus on using customer information and rewards to encourage repeat purchases.

**Relevant features:**
- Point of Sale
- Customer management
- Customer purchase history
- Loyalty points
- Discounts
- Inventory management
- Sales reporting

### 6.2 Odoo Point of Sale

Odoo provides a Point-of-Sale system with customer loyalty and discount features.

Its loyalty functionality allows businesses to create reward programs, discounts and other customer incentives. Customers can earn points and use rewards during purchases.

**Relevant features:**
